**Nandini Nair** (2025–26) studied chiral bosons in two dimensions, bosonisation duality, and chiral anomalies.
She is now a PhD scholar at IIT Gandhinagar.

**Pallavi Chauhan** (2025–26) wrote her dissertations on quantum field theory for many-body systems.
In [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wM3kQ53iESxKFUy5avT69hXXBGxdBax3/view?usp=sharing), she studied examples of many-body systems in quantum mechanics and field theory.
In [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gUa6zYXipVtljiou_D9OqVAxQSB5GGHn/view?usp=sharing), she studied Fermi liquids and diagrammatic methods at zero temperature.

**Md. Albadar Qamar** (2025–26) wrote his dissertation on topological effects in condensed matter systems.
In [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1hSPjTEA9xrckeO-1zEuS_hnvNkLRFXU1/view?usp=sharing), he studied topological insulators and the integer quantum Hall effect.
In [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OHwwuztPCbsW8UmiR4bYYC7JIvZenp58/view?usp=sharing), he studied various aspects of Chern-Simons theory.
He is currently a research assistant at HRI, Allahabad.

**Preksha Uniyal** (2025–26) wrote her dissertation on lattice approaches to quantum field theory.
In [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vRE0B5UsGLI_8sp1ONG-hKyc_uoEzvgm/view?usp=sharing), she studied scalar fields and fermions on the lattice.
In [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1WNsrDoRqccBFity6AEmTRv1SsBZD9s1z/view?usp=sharing), she studied gauge theories on the lattice.

**Shashank Sengar** (2024–25) wrote his dissertation on Lie algebras and conformal field theories.
In [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fpoxq9WCjNSSJF0l-LgNOrWj3MgySySq/view?usp=sharing), he studied Lie algebras and their classification.
In [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Qx4sWrYMmpm2TK2Tj9zuGb6hIG8wfLLM/view?usp=sharing), he studied two-dimensional conformal field theories.
He is now a PhD scholar at the Chennai Mathematical Institute.

**Bharti Sangwan** (2024–25) wrote her dissertation on integrability and stochastic processes.
In [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1gbHaPfevE8CsXqhZtu3wcOZg3Q3ScqRu/view?usp=sharing), she studied some aspects of classical integrability.
In [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13hVrvtYb4o9w9i9WTlyFBfculH0WHdxh/view?usp=sharing), she studied stochastic differential equations and Brownian motion.
She is now a PhD scholar at NISER Bhubaneshwar.

**Nihit Agarwal** (2024–25) wrote his dissertation on the worldline approach to quantum field theory.
His notes based on these studies can be found in [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dQ2JGr56tFOhzGb0yYAxIUM-a3qbgfKX/view?usp=sharing) and [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1H9FJ2znkB_m2GMaNprILOAMn8J99-IC6/view?usp=sharing) of his MSc dissertation.
He is now a PhD scholar at IISER Bhopal.

**Shubham Tiwari** (2024) studied the bootstrap approach to determining the spectrum of quantum mechanical systems.
He is now a PhD scholar at the University of Delhi.

**Aditi Shahani** (2023–24) studied the chiral ring of some four-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theories.
Her work led to [this paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01%282025%29056).
She is now a PhD scholar at the University of Oklahoma.

**Vishal Sharma** (2023–24) studied aspects of supersymmetric quantum mechanics, path integrals, instantons, and some elementary Morse theory.

**Surabhi Srivastava** (2023–24) wrote her dissertation on phase transitions, scaling behaviour, and the renormalisation of statistical field theories.
Her notes based on these studies can be found in [Part I](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1G5UAYsT6yZ9tD134ghOMGjeuoMSEEKnG/view?usp=sharing) and [Part II](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XKJ0ebThhlWyMTg8Tx6w2a5VYyKhySNz/view?usp=sharing) of her MSc dissertation.
She is now a PhD scholar at IISER Bhopal.
