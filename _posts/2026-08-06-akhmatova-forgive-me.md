On p. 622 of _The Complete Poems of Anna Akhmatova: Expanded Edition_
(1992) with translations by Judith Hemschemeyer and edited by Roberta
Reeder, we read that Anna Akhmatova authored, in 1925, the following 
verse:

> And you will forgive me everything:\
> Even that I am not young,\
> And even that baseless slander,\
> Like putrid smoke with a blessed flame,\
> Has been fused forever with my name.

In 1927, she revisited this motif.

> Forgive me, that I manage badly,\
> Manage badly but live gloriously,\
> That I leave traces of myself in my songs,\
> That I appeared to you in waking dreams.\
> Forgive me, though you'll never know\
> That forever with my name,\
> Baseless slander has entwined itself,\
> Like acrid smoke with lively flame.
