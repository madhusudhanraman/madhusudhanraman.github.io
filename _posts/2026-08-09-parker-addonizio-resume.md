Dorothy Parker in _Enough Rope_ (1926).

> Razors pain you;\
> Rivers are damp;\
> Acids stain you;\
> And drugs cause cramp.\
> Guns aren't lawful;\
> Nooses give;\
> Gas smells awful;\
> You might as well live.

Kim Addonizio in _Now We're Getting Somewhere_ (2022). 

> Families shame you;\
> Rehab's a scam;\
> Lovers drain you\
> And don’t give a damn.\
> Friends are distracted;\
> Aging stinks;\
> You'll soon be subtracted;\
> You might as well drink.
