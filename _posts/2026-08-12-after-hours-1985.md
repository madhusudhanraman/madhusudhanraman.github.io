With everyone gushing over Christopher Nolan's _Odyssey_, and since my
curiosity (morbid, perhaps) about this film is not powerful enough to
actually move me to go watch it, I thought I might watch instead an
older film, this one also about a man trying to get back home.

I don't have much to say about this film, except that Scorsese does an
excellent job of balancing the tension and humour. Every time you
think Paul Hackett (Griffin Dunne) is going to catch a break, some
fresh nonsense erupts, frustrating this expectation. Like Odysseus,
Hackett returns to where he started out from, waylaid instead by
masochistic sculptors, world-weary bartenders, lonely and vengeful
waitresses, lunatic club-goers, an ice-cream truck driver, a vigilante
mob, and two hapless thieves.
