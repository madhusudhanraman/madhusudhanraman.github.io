When Brautigan was poet-in-residence at the California Institute of
Technology, he (understandably) wrote:

> I don't care how God-damn smart\
> these guys are: I'm bored.
> 
> It's been raining like hell all day long\
> and there's nothing to do.

The phrase "machines of loving grace" is used often these days, and
derives from the title poem in the collection _All Watched Over by
Machines of Loving Grace_ (1967). It amuses me greatly to note that he
was writing stuff like this too, around the same time.
