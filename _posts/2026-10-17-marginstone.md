I’ve been building a small Mac app for keeping passages from things I read. It is called [Marginstone](https://github.com/madhusudhanraman/marginstone). The idea came from [Varun’s Buddy](https://github.com/varun-ml/buddy), which I acknowledge with delight and eternal gratitude.

A small companion sits on the screen, and you drop passages onto it. These are saved locally; you can edit them, organise them by source, and export them later. There is also an option to publish them to a website, though you need neither a website nor a GitHub account to use the app. The companions are fossils: a nautilus, a leaf, an ammonite, a brachiopod, and a fern. Fragments preserved, I suppose, although I also simply liked how they looked.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/marginstone-companions.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Nautilus, Leaf, Ammonite, Brachiopod, and Fern" style="display: block; width: 100%; height: auto;">

There are rough edges, particularly around identifying sources and capturing from different browsers. If you try it, I would be interested to hear what works, what doesn’t, and what seems unnecessarily complicated.
